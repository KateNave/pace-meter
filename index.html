    How it works. The meter finds syllables by listening for bursts of loudness: every vowel sound makes one, and each burst is a tick. Some voices and microphones blur two syllables into one burst, or split one into two. Reading a passage with a known number of syllables shows how far off the detector is for you, and the meter corrects for it from then on.
