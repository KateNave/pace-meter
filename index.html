    Settings

    Counts the syllables in your voice and converts them to words per minute, so it is an estimate. Calibrate once for the best accuracy. Nothing you say leaves your phone.

    Uses Chrome's speech recognition, which on Android may send audio to Google to transcribe. Pace is only as accurate as the recognition; mumbled or very fast words can be missed. Some phones can't record at the same time.

    Calibrate to your voice

    How it works. The meter doesn't hear words, only syllables: every vowel sound makes a short burst of loudness, and each burst is one tick. Reading a passage with a known number of words shows how many syllables you say per word. From then on, words per minute = syllables per minute ÷ that number.

    Tap Start, read the passage aloud at your normal speaking pace, then tap Stop.
